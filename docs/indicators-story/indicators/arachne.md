# ARACHNE+ indicators

A flag or score is a reason to review a project, contract, beneficiary, or entity. It is not proof of fraud, corruption, irregularity, or illegality, and ARACHNE+ does not make automatic decisions.

## Inputs publicly identified by the Commission

ARACHNE+ combines operational data supplied by EU institutions and Member States with external data. Publicly described inputs include:

- beneficiaries, contractors, subcontractors, project partners, service providers, and consortium members;
- roles, identifiers, addresses, turnover, contract details, and financial flows;
- key experts and other involved persons;
- final or ultimate beneficial owners;
- Orbis company ownership, financial, and corporate-structure information; and
- World Compliance PEP, sanctions, enforcement, and adverse-media information.

## Use in the Lithuanian catalogue

ARACHNE+ is an important supporting reference for EU-funded Lithuanian projects, particularly for ownership, relationship, PEP, sanctions, reputation, concentration, eligibility, and financial-flow risks. It is not interchangeable with OCP or OpenTender: ARACHNE+ scores projects and entities using controlled and commercially enriched information, while the other catalogues primarily provide reproducible procurement-data indicators.

No `ARACHNE-*` entry should be treated as an executable rule until an authorized ARACHNE+ user verifies the current indicator name, definition, level of analysis, formula, threshold, weight, version, and permitted use inside the system.
