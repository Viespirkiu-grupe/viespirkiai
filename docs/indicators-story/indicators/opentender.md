# OpenTender indicators

A flag is a reason to review a procurement, not proof of fraud, corruption, or illegality.

Source: [iMonitor 2.0, *Corruption Risk Indicators in Public Procurement: An updated opentender.eu framework* (2025)](https://imonitor.govtransparency.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/D2.2-Updated-Risk-Assessment-Methodology_final.pdf), §§6.5–6.6. The composite Average Integrity Indicator Score is excluded because it aggregates rather than adds a twelfth individual indicator.
