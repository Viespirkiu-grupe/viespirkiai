# OLAF-supported Red Flags indicators

A flag is a reason to review a procurement, not proof of fraud, corruption, or illegality.

Source: [*Towards More Integrity: “Red Flags” – a New Automatic Warning System*](https://transparency.lt/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/OLAF_Red_Flags_Booklet.pdf), p. 8. The booklet was produced by an OLAF-supported project; these are not represented here as an official OLAF-wide mandatory methodology. The source summary contains 32 contract-notice rows, but marks “duration of evaluation” as no longer an indicator; therefore the operational count is **31 + 9 = 40**.
