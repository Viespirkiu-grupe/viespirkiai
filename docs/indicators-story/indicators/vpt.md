# VPT indicators

A flag is a reason to review a procurement, not proof of fraud, corruption, or illegality.

Normalized from the [VPT public-procurement efficiency monitoring indicators](https://vpt.lrv.lt/lt/statistika-ir-analize/viesuju-pirkimu-efektyvumo-stebesenos-rodikliai/) and Švieslentė measures. These are catalogue labels, not official VPT codes; dashboard definitions and reporting periods can change.

| VPT-I03 | Share of green procurements |

| VPT-I05 | Share of procurements applying quality criteria |
| VPT-I06 | Share of electronic procurements |

| VPT-I13 | Share of procurements receiving supplier complaints |
| VPT-I14 | Share of procurements challenged in court |
| VPT-I15 | Share/value of non-competitive procedures |
